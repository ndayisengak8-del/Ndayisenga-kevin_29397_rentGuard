## RentGuard Rwanda – Research Log
# 1. Introduction

This research looks at the challenges faced by landlords and tenants when managing rental properties. It focuses on problems such as recording rent payments, preparing monthly rent bills, keeping tenant information and identifying tenants who have not paid their rent.
The research also looks at existing property management systems and how Oracle Database and PL/SQL can help manage rental information.

# 3. Research Gap

From the research, we found that rental property management involves several activities, including managing properties, registering tenants, preparing rent bills and recording payments.
Existing systems such as Buildium provide property management services, while the selected Kaggle dataset mainly provides information about rental properties.
However, our project will focus on bringing important rental management activities together in one Oracle database.

## RentGuard Rwanda will manage:

A. Property and unit information.

B. Landlord and tenant records.

C. Rental agreements and lease periods
.
D. Monthly rent invoices.

E. Tenant payments.

F. Unpaid rent and rental arrears.
The project will use Oracle Database and PL/SQL to store information and manage these activities.

# 4. Conclusion

The research has helped us understand the challenges of rental property management and the importance of keeping accurate records.
Based on the information collected, we will develop RentGuard Rwanda as a database system that helps manage rental properties, tenants, leases, rent invoices and payments.
The system will also help landlords identify unpaid rent and keep track of rental payment records.
